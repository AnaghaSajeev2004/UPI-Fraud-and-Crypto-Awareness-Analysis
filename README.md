## 📌 Project Overview

Digital payment platforms such as UPI have become an essential part of everyday financial transactions in India. However, the increasing use of digital payments has also created opportunities for online fraud and financial scams.

This project focuses on analyzing UPI fraud patterns, financial losses, scam techniques, recovery rates, and digital financial awareness among young people.

Using Power BI, I developed an interactive 4-page dashboard to explore national UPI fraud patterns and youth awareness of digital finance and cryptocurrency-related risks.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Identify the most common UPI fraud types and scam techniques.
- Analyze financial losses and fraud recovery rates.
- Identify high-risk age groups and states.
- Understand the relationship between scam awareness and fraud victimization.
- Analyze cryptocurrency awareness and ownership among youth.
- Compare youth survey findings with national UPI fraud patterns.

## 📂 Datasets Used

| Dataset | Records | Description |
|---|---:|---|
| UPI Fraud India Dataset | 1,000 | UPI fraud transactions, fraud types, amounts, states, apps and recovery status |
| Digital Finance Survey Dataset | 150 | Youth survey covering UPI usage, scam awareness, fraud experience and cryptocurrency attitudes |

**Note:** The youth survey dataset is synthetic (simulated) and is used for demonstration purposes. Its findings should not be interpreted as actual population statistics.

## 🔍 Key Insights

- The analyzed dataset contains 1,000 reported UPI fraud cases.
- Total financial loss is approximately ₹15.9 million.
- Phishing (21.8%) and Vishing (18.8%) are among the most common fraud types.
- KYC update, prize/lottery wins and refund processing are prominent scam lures.
- The 26–35 age group records the highest number of fraud cases.
- 68% of cases were not recovered, while only 10% were fully recovered.
- Only 47% of national cases were reported to the cybercrime portal.

**Youth Survey Insights (Synthetic Data):**
- Fraud victimization decreases as scam awareness increases.
- Instagram and YouTube are the leading sources of financial information.
- 84% of respondents indicate demand for financial awareness training.
- Cryptocurrency owners perceive cryptocurrency as safer than non-owners in the survey.

## 💡 Recommendations

1. Conduct digital payment awareness programs in colleges.
2. Educate users about phishing, vishing and fake customer-care scams.
3. Encourage users never to share UPI PINs or OTPs.
4. Promote immediate reporting of financial fraud through the 1930 cybercrime helpline.
5. Improve awareness about cryptocurrency scams and misleading financial advice on social media.

## ⚠️ Project Limitations

- The youth survey contains 150 synthetic records.
- Survey insights are illustrative and require validation using real survey data.
- National fraud data and youth survey data are compared by distribution, not at the individual level.
